Delete your Bríq account

This page explains how to delete your Bríq account and what happens to your data when you do.

App: Bríq
Developer: Bríq, Ireland

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Bríq app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings. You will find it on the Profile tab under General, or on the More tab. The screen that opens is titled Settings & Privacy.
  3. Scroll to the Privacy & Security section and tap Your Data Rights.
  4. On the Your Data Rights screen, find the Delete Your Account section.
  5. Tap Request Account Deletion.
  6. A message titled Request Account Deletion appears. Tap Request Deletion to confirm.

If you use multi-factor authentication, you will be asked to verify who you are before the request is sent.

What happens after you ask us to delete it

Your account is not deleted straight away. It is scheduled for deletion 30 days later.

You can change your mind during those 30 days. Sign back in, go to Settings & Privacy → Privacy & Security → Your Data Rights, and tap Cancel Deletion. Your account then carries on as normal.

When the 30 days are up, we delete your account. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted

When the 30 days end, your sign-in account is deleted, and everything linked to it goes with it:

  • your profile and account details, including your name and email address;
  • your budgets, transactions and savings goals;
  • your bills and reminders;
  • your tax and calculator history;
  • your children’s profiles and all Kids Zone progress;
  • your consent choices;
  • your subscription record held in the app;
  • records of failed sign-in attempts that used your email address.

None of this can be recovered afterwards.

What is kept, for how long, and why

We keep a small number of records, but your user identifier is removed from them first, so they are no longer linked to you or to your email address:

  • Security records — for example a record that a password was changed or that a sign-in looked unusual. Kept so we can investigate security problems and show that we handled them properly.
  • Activity and audit records — a log that an action happened. Kept so we can meet our legal duty to show how data in the app has been handled.
  • App performance records — for example how long a screen took to load. Kept so we can keep the app working well.

These records are kept for as long as we need them for security, legal and app-reliability reasons. Because your identifier has been removed, they cannot be traced back to you.

Two things sit outside our control, so please deal with them yourself:

  • Your subscription. If you pay for Bríq, cancel it in the App Store or Google Play. Deleting your Bríq account does not cancel a subscription you bought through a store.
  • Your bank connection. If you linked a bank account, disconnect it in the app before you delete your account.

Our full retention schedule is set out in the Privacy Policy.

If you cannot sign in to the app

You can still ask us to delete your account.

Email privacy@briq.ie from the email address you used for your Bríq account, and ask us to delete the account.

We handle these requests by hand. Sending the email from your own account address lets us check that the account really is yours before we delete anything. We may ask you to confirm a code we send to that address.

Requests made this way follow the same 30-day schedule as requests made in the app.

Questions

For anything about your data, email privacy@briq.ie. For general help, email support@briq.ie.

You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at www.dataprotection.ie.
