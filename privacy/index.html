Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 16 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bríq (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Bríq is designed and built in Ireland, by Irish, for an Irish audience. We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Irish data protection laws.

2. Data Controller

Patrick Carroll is the data controller responsible for your personal data, and operates the Bríq app.

Contact us:
Email: privacy@briq.ie

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Give Us

  • Your name and email address, to create your account
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Financial information you enter: income and tax details, budgets, transactions, savings goals, debts, pensions, insurance policies and bills
  • A first name and an age band for each child profile you set up in Kids Zone

3.2 Your Circumstances, If You Use the Supports Checker

The supports checker works out which Irish payments and schemes you may be entitled to. It only has what you type into it, and every answer is optional — you can skip any question and still use the rest of the app.

  • Your age, marital status, employment status and housing status
  • How many dependants you have, and how many are children
  • Whether you have a partner, and whether you are a single parent
  • Whether you pay rent
  • Your income band, and your gross annual income if you give it

Your county — approximate location. You can tell us which county you live in, because some supports are county-specific. This is a county you pick from a list yourself. It is never taken from your device’s location, and never worked out from your IP address. The app asks for no location permission and has none.

⚠ Medical card — health-related data. You can tell us whether you hold a medical card, because it decides entitlement to several supports. Under the GDPR this counts as special category data concerning health (Article 9), so we are flagging it plainly rather than burying it in a list. It is entirely optional, it is used only to work out which supports to show you, it is never shared with anyone, and you can clear it at any time. Leave it blank if you would rather not tell us.

3.3 Information We Record When You Use the App

  • Security and activity records: the action taken, the time, your IP address and your device’s browser identification string
  • Diagnostic reports when the app hits an error or crashes
  • The name of a CSV file you import and how many rows it had

We do not measure how you use features, and we do not build usage profiles or audience reports. The app contains no measurement or advertising software of any kind.

Reminders never leave your phone. Bill and reminder notifications are scheduled on your own device and delivered by it. Nothing is registered with Apple, Google or Expo to make them work, and nothing about your reminders is sent anywhere in order to deliver them.

3.4 Consent Records

  • Your consent choices
  • The IP address and time at which you gave or changed them
  • The version of the wording you agreed to, and your language

3.5 Things You Send Us or Attach

Receipt photos. If you attach a receipt to a transaction, we store only a reference to that photo on your device — its location in your phone’s storage. The image itself is never uploaded and we never receive it.

CSV imports. When you import transactions from a CSV file, the file is read and processed on your device. We store the file’s name, how many rows it contained and whether the import succeeded — never the file’s contents.

Contact form. If you write to us through the contact form, we store your name, your email address, the subject and your message, so that we can answer you. See section 7 for how long we keep it.

Broker enquiries. See section 6.1 — this is the one case where something you write is sent to someone outside our processors, and it only happens when you ask for it.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract: to provide the app — your account, tax calculations, budgets, bills and Kids Zone
  • Consent: for marketing emails, for setting up a child profile, for sending an enquiry to a broker, and for the optional circumstances you give the supports checker
  • Legitimate Interest: to keep the app secure, to investigate suspected misuse of accounts, and to find and fix faults
  • Legal Obligation: to keep the records data protection law requires us to keep

⚠ Health-related data. Whether you hold a medical card is special category data under Article 9 of the GDPR. We rely on your explicit consent for it, under Article 9(2)(a), and on nothing else. You never have to answer it, and the supports checker works without it.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing it does not undo anything we did while it was in force.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Process tax calculations and financial insights
  • Track Kids Zone progress and achievements
  • Send you reminders you have asked for, such as a bill falling due
  • Send important updates about the app
  • Find and fix faults, using diagnostic reports
  • Respond to your requests and support enquiries
  • Send marketing communications (with consent)
  • Keep the app and your account secure
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Service providers. We use the processors below, each under a data processing agreement, and share only the data each one needs to do its job. This is the complete list.

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in system and backend. Holds your account details, all of your app data, and your audit and consent records. Stored in the EU region.
  • Postmark — sends our authentication emails, such as confirming your address and resetting your password. Receives your email address and the content of that email.
  • Apple — provides Sign in with Apple. If you use it, Apple handles the sign-in and tells us only what you release to us, which may be a private relay email address rather than your own.
  • Google — provides Sign in with Google. If you use it, Google handles the sign-in and gives us your Google account name and email address.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and purchases. Receives your account identifier and your purchase and entitlement data.
  • Expo — the pipeline that builds and releases the app. It receives no personal data while you use the app; your reminders are scheduled and delivered by your own device.
  • Sentry — crash and error monitoring. Receives diagnostic error reports; configured not to attach personal identifiers, IP addresses, or device identifiers, with additional server-side scrubbing of personal data before events are stored.
  • Resend — delivers the emails you choose to send through the app: broker enquiries, parental consent requests, and partner applications. Receives the recipient’s address, the content you compose (which may include financial figures you have entered), and your own email address.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech narration for Kids Zone lessons. Receives the lesson text to be converted to audio, and no personal data.
  • ipify — a lookup service that tells us the public IP address your device is using, so that we can record it in the security and activity records described in section 3.3. Receives your IP address.

We also disclose personal data in these situations:

  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger or acquisition
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly agree to share data

6.1 Sending an Enquiry to a Broker

The app can send a mortgage or insurance enquiry to brokers you pick from a list. This is the one place where something you have written is sent to a company that is not one of our processors, so it works like this:

  • You choose the brokers. Nothing is sent to anyone you did not select.
  • You must turn on consent first. There is a switch reading “Share these figures with the brokers I selected”. It is off by default and never pre-ticked, and the send button stays disabled until you turn it on.
  • What is sent: the message as you wrote it, including any calculated figures it contains, and your email address so the broker can reply.
  • How it travels: through our server and then through Resend, our email provider, to the brokers you selected.
  • A copy to yourself is sent only if you leave that option on.
  • Once a broker has your enquiry, what they do with it is covered by their privacy policy, not ours.

6.2 Bank Accounts

This version of the app cannot connect to a bank account. Open banking is not part of this release: there is no way to reach it in the app, no bank is contacted, and no account, balance or transaction data is ever requested or received. If we add it later we will name the provider here and ask for your consent before anything is connected.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

While your account is open, we keep your data for as long as you have an account, so that the app can show it back to you.

When you ask us to delete your account, we schedule the deletion for 30 days later. You can cancel it in the app at any point during those 30 days. When the 30 days are up, your sign-in account is deleted and everything attached to it is deleted with it, including your profile, your budgets, transactions, savings goals, bills and reminders, your calculator history, your child profiles and all Kids Zone progress, your subscription record, and your consent records. None of it is recoverable, and nothing is held back for a later date.

Full instructions, including how to ask us if you can no longer sign in, are at https://briq.ie/delete-account/

Records we keep after deletion. Three kinds of record are not attached to your account and so are not deleted with it: security records, activity and audit records, and app performance records. Your user identifier is removed from them when your account goes.

⚠ You should know that removing the identifier does not make these records anonymous. They still contain the IP address and the device browser identification string recorded at the time, and a free-text description of what happened, and those can point back to a person. We are telling you this rather than describing the records as anonymous, because they are not. We keep them for 12 months from the day each record is created and then delete them. We keep them that long to investigate security incidents and to show a regulator how data in the app has been handled.

Contact form messages. When you write to us through the contact form we keep your name, email address, subject and message for 24 months so that we have a record of the enquiry and our answer, and then we delete them.

Parental consent requests. These are recorded in two steps. When the request is sent, we store the parent’s email address, the child’s age and the time. Only when the parent approves it do we also record the IP address and device information of the approval — that is what evidences who gave the consent. A request that is never approved never gains either. The record is kept for 12 months and then deleted. It is not attached to your account, so it is not removed by deleting your account; email us if you want it erased sooner.

One thing we cannot delete for you. A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play is cancelled there, not here — deleting your Bríq account does not cancel it.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Right to Object: Object to certain processing activities
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, go to Settings → Your Data Rights, where you can see, export and delete your data yourself. You can also email privacy@briq.ie. We answer within one month.

Deleting your account: you can delete your account in the app at any time via Settings → Your Data Rights. Instructions are also published at https://briq.ie/delete-account/, including how to request deletion if you can no longer sign in.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Encryption at rest for stored data
  • Secure token storage using platform-specific secure storage
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on database tables, so one account cannot read another’s data
  • Optional multi-factor authentication and biometric unlock on your account
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Your account and app data held on EU-based servers (Supabase EU region) — see section 10 for how other processors are handled

10. International Data Transfers

Your primary personal data — your account, your app data, and your audit and consent records — is stored in the EU region of our backend provider, Supabase.

Some of the other processors listed in section 6 may process limited data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where that occurs, the transfer is governed by appropriate safeguards — such as Standard Contractual Clauses — under our data processing agreements with those providers.

11. Children’s Privacy and Kids Zone

Kids Zone teaches children about money. A child does not have their own account: a parent or guardian sets up a child profile inside their own account, and the parent stays in control of it.

Parental consent. Under GDPR Article 8 a child cannot give valid consent on their own, and Ireland sets the digital age of consent at 16. So for anyone under 16, a parent or guardian must consent before we create a child profile or handle any of the child’s information. We ask for that consent by email to the parent, and we record that it was given — see section 7 for exactly what is stored and when.

What we hold about a child. Only what Kids Zone needs:

  • A first name or nickname, whatever the parent types in
  • An age band — 6–8, 9–11 or 12–14 — not a date of birth
  • A chosen avatar picture
  • Progress: points, level, streak, lessons finished, badges earned, quiz and challenge counts, and the pretend money balance used in the lessons

We do not ask for a child’s surname, date of birth, address, photograph, school or contact details. A child profile cannot sign in, cannot send a message, and cannot be contacted through the app. Lesson narration audio is generated from the lesson text only, and no child’s information is sent to produce it.

Who can delete it. The parent or guardian whose account holds the profile can delete a child profile at any time in Kids Zone, which erases that child’s progress with it. Deleting the parent’s account deletes every child profile in it. A parent can also email privacy@briq.ie to have a child’s data erased, and can withdraw their consent at any time.

12. Local Storage and Tracking

Bríq is a mobile app, not a website. It does not set browser cookies. This section explains what is stored on your device, what diagnostic data we collect, and how you control it.

12.1 Storage on Your Device

The app stores some information locally on your device so that it can work at all:

  • Login session: your authentication tokens, held in your device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), so you stay signed in between app launches
  • App settings: your preferences, including language and accessibility options
  • App data: information needed for the app to display your budgets, calculations and progress

This storage is required for the app to function. It stays on your device, it is not used to track you, and it is not shared with advertisers.

12.2 Diagnostics

When the app encounters an error or crashes, a diagnostic report is sent to Sentry, our error-monitoring processor described in section 6. Sentry is configured not to attach personal identifiers, IP addresses or device identifiers, and reports are additionally scrubbed of personal data before they are stored. Diagnostic data is kept only as long as needed to investigate and fix the fault.

12.3 No Advertising or Cross-App Tracking

Bríq contains no advertising. We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and the app contains no third-party advertising, measurement or tracking software. We do not profile you, and no decision about you is made automatically.

12.4 Managing Your Consent and Your Data

You control how your data is used, and you can change your choices at any time:

  • Consent: go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Consent Preferences to review or change the consents you have given
  • Your data: go to Settings → Your Data Rights to access, export or delete your data
  • Notifications: go to Settings → Notifications, or turn them off for Bríq in your phone’s own settings

Withdrawing a consent does not affect the parts of the app that need your data to work, such as staying signed in or saving your budgets.
