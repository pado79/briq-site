Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 6 September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bríq (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Bríq is designed and built in Ireland, by Irish, for an Irish audience. We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Irish data protection laws.

2. Data Controller

Bríq is the data controller responsible for your personal data.

Contact us:
Email: support@briq.ie
Address: Ireland

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Personal Information

  • Name and email address (for account creation)
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Financial data you enter for tax calculations
  • Kids Zone progress and achievements

3.2 Usage Information

  • App usage patterns and feature interactions
  • Device information (model, OS version, unique identifiers)
  • Log data (IP address, access times, pages viewed)
  • Analytics data (with your consent)

3.3 Consent Records

  • Your consent preferences
  • IP address and timestamp of consent
  • Consent version and locale

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract: To provide core app functionality (account management, tax calculations, Kids Zone features)
  • Consent: For marketing communications and third-party data sharing
  • Legitimate Interest: For analytics and app improvement
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with tax and financial regulations

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Process tax calculations and financial insights
  • Track Kids Zone progress and achievements
  • Send important updates about the app
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Respond to your requests and support inquiries
  • Send marketing communications (with consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: we use the following processors, each under a data processing agreement, and share only the data necessary for their function:
  • Supabase — database, authentication and backend (personal data, app data, and audit/consent records). Stored in the EU region.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management. Receives your account identifier and purchase/entitlement data.
  • Sentry — crash and error monitoring. Receives diagnostic error reports; configured not to attach personal identifiers, IP addresses, or device identifiers, with additional server-side scrubbing of personal data before events are stored.
  • Resend — delivery of broker enquiry emails you choose to send. Receives the recipient broker address(es), the enquiry content you compose (which may include financial figures you have entered), and your own email address only if you opt to copy yourself.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech narration for Kids Zone lessons. Receives the lesson text to be converted to audio.
  • Expo (Expo Application Services) — the build and release pipeline for the app.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger or acquisition
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly agree to share data

7. Data Retention

  • Profile Data: 12 months after account deletion
  • Financial Calculations: 12 months after account deletion
  • Kids Progress: 12 months after account deletion
  • Usage Analytics: 24 months
  • Audit Logs: Anonymized and retained indefinitely for compliance
  • Consent Records: Retained indefinitely for legal compliance

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Right to Object: Object to certain processing activities
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, visit Settings → Your Data Rights or contact privacy@briq.ie

Deleting your account: you can delete your account in the app at any time via Settings → Your Data Rights. Instructions are also published at https://briq.ie/delete-account/, including how to request deletion if you can no longer sign in.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Encryption at rest for stored data
  • Secure token storage using platform-specific secure storage
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on database tables
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Your account and app data held on EU-based servers (Supabase EU region) — see section 10 for how other processors are handled

10. International Data Transfers

Your primary personal data — your account, your app data, and your audit and consent records — is stored in the EU region of our backend provider, Supabase.

Some of the other processors listed in section 6 may process limited data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where that occurs, the transfer is governed by appropriate safeguards — such as Standard Contractual Clauses — under our data processing agreements with those providers.

11. Children’s Privacy

The Kids Zone feature is designed for children to learn about money management under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. Parents control their children’s accounts and data.

12. Local Storage, Analytics and Tracking

Bríq is a mobile app, not a website. It does not set browser cookies. This section explains what is stored on your device, what diagnostic data we collect, and how you control it.

12.1 Storage on Your Device

The app stores some information locally on your device so that it can work at all:

  • Login session: your authentication tokens, held in your device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), so you stay signed in between app launches
  • App settings: your preferences, including language and accessibility options
  • App data: information needed for the app to display your budgets, calculations and progress

This storage is required for the app to function. It stays on your device, it is not used to track you, and it is not shared with advertisers.

12.2 Diagnostics

When the app encounters an error or crashes, a diagnostic report is sent to Sentry, our error-monitoring processor described in section 6. Sentry is configured not to attach personal identifiers, IP addresses or device identifiers, and reports are additionally scrubbed of personal data before they are stored.

12.3 No Advertising or Cross-App Tracking

Bríq contains no advertising. We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not include any third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

12.4 Managing Your Consent and Your Data

You control how your data is used, and you can change your choices at any time:

  • Consent: go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Consent Preferences to review or change your analytics and data-processing consent
  • Your data: go to Settings → Your Data Rights to access, export or delete your data

Withdrawing analytics consent does not affect the parts of the app that need your data to work, such as staying signed in or saving your budgets. Diagnostic data is retained only as long as needed to investigate and fix faults.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
